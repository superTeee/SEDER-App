Features

Everything for the cigar enthusiast

From identifying an unknown cigar to keeping your collection in perfect shape.

📷

AI scanning

Snap the band and Vitola recognizes the cigar automatically — with on-device text reading and an AI fallback.

💧

Humidor with RH

Log relative humidity per humidor, set a target and range, and see a calm traffic-light status.

📖

Tasting journal

Rate draw, burn and flavor, add a score and notes, and build your own taste profile over time.

🔎

Explore

Search thousands of cigars by brand, series, shape and flavor notes — with users' top 3 and a daily pick.

👥

Feed & friends

Share smokes in a social feed, follow friends, comment and like — and discover new favorites.

🗄️

Humidor management

Multiple humidors, move cigars between them, count down as you smoke, and stay organized.

Terms

Privacy & terms

In short: we only collect what the app needs, never sell your data, and don't use it for advertising.

What we collect

Email address (for sign-in), name/nickname (optional), the cigar notes and ratings you enter yourself, and city/location (optional).

What we don't collect

Health data, payment information, location tracking, or third-party data without your consent.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to run the Vitola app: to store and show your cigar journal, identify your account and let you sign in. We do not sell your data to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Who sees your data

Data is stored in Supabase (USA). We do not share data with other parties. Sign-in via Apple or Google is handled by Apple and Google respectively under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your data at any time. Delete your account directly in the app under Settings → Delete account.

Age limit & tobacco

Vitola is intended only for users aged 18 and over. The app does not sell tobacco and does not run tobacco advertising — it is a tool to organize and log your own collection.
